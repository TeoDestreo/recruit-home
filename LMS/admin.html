Captions & transcript

Upload a caption file (.vtt or .srt) to show captions on the video; a transcript is made from it automatically. To show your own wording instead, also upload a transcript (.txt). Learners with access see the transcript under the video. YouTube videos use YouTube's own captions, and the transcript still appears.

Lumi interactive activity

Create your activity in Lumi Desktop, then save/export a .h5p file with its libraries included. Upload that file here—not a standalone HTML file or SCORM ZIP. There is no built-in Lumi editor on this site.

Use bundled media, not external embeds. Maximum: 60 MB compressed, 128 MB expanded, 16 MB per file, 2,000 files. Keep large videos in the course video slot.

After uploading: open Preview, finish the full activity, and look for “Completion tracking verified.” Then attach it and choose whether it is required before the test. Some H5P types do not report completion; those cannot be made required until verified.
